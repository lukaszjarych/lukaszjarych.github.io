My work spans the full path from fragmented source data to usable reporting. That includes structuring data, modeling business logic, improving quality and maintainability, and turning technical complexity into outputs that business users can actually trust and act on.

One of my strongest advantages is how quickly I learn new tools, domains, and business contexts. Over the years I have worked in startups, mid-sized companies, and larger corporate environments, which taught me how to adapt my approach without losing delivery quality. I am comfortable communicating across teams and countries, working with both technical and business stakeholders, and turning unclear requirements into something structured and useful.

I am strongest where analytics engineering and BI delivery meet: SQL transformation, semantic modeling, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, dbt-style thinking, automation, and stakeholder-facing delivery. I enjoy building systems that are not only technically correct, but also clear, reusable, and stable enough to support recurring decision-making.

Another differentiator is that I do not work only as a dashboard builder or only as a data developer. I also bring business analyst and systems analyst thinking into my work. That helps me ask better questions, understand process and data dependencies, define KPIs more clearly, and connect business needs with practical technical design.

This portfolio is designed to show more than dashboards. I want employers to see how I think about reliable analytics systems: data flows, transformation layers, metric logic, governance, model design, and the tradeoffs required to move from vague business questions to practical reporting.

I bring a mix of hands-on delivery and analytical structure. That means shaping reporting layers around real business questions, helping teams align on definitions, and moving from inconsistent data and unclear requirements toward useful, decision-ready outputs.

Much of my professional work is confidential, so this portfolio uses selected sanitized visuals and carefully written case studies to stay accurate about the technical work without exposing sensitive implementation details.
